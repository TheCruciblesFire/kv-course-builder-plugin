## Section Selection Guidance

Select only sections warranted by the source. Do not force every workbook to include every possible section. Prefer fewer, clearer prompts over many repetitive questions.

Good workbook sections may include:

- Read / Review
- What the Text Says
- What This Means
- Why This Matters
- Think It Through
- Personal Reflection
- Practice This
- Prayer / Response
- Next Step
